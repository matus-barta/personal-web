I hope this will be self-hosted service for Management and Monitoring of Windows, Linux and Mac.

- **Tech stack (not everything is set in stone):**
- **API**: _Typescript_, _Postgres_, _Node_, _Express_, _Prisma_, _Zod_
- **Web**: _SvelteKit_, _Tailwind_, _Typescript_, _Prisma_, _Postgres_
- **Agent**: _Rust_, eventually _Tauri_ for GUI
