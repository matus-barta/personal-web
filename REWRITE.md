# Rewrite plan

What is left of the site's rewrite: the features and the pages. The text they show - the posts, the projects and About - is the README's "Content" list - the new pages too, each built once its text is written - and what waits for another project is its "Waiting on upstream".

Each group below is one pull request, in this order; a big one can split. A checkbox is a task. **Decide** marks a choice to make before the tasks that depend on it. Facts were checked on 2026-10-24: claims about this repository name the file they come from, and claims about anything else link their source.

## 1. Light mode

Dark stays the default for everyone, whatever their system asks for; light is an option for accessibility or a strong preference, chosen with a toggle.

- [ ] **Wiring:** [`mode-watcher`](https://github.com/svecosystem/mode-watcher) 1.1.0, the latest, as shadcn-svelte's [dark mode docs](https://shadcn-svelte.com/docs/dark-mode/svelte) use: `<ModeWatcher defaultMode="dark" />` in the root layout. Its head script sets `.dark` and `color-scheme` on `<html>` from `localStorage` before the first paint, so a prerendered page doesn't flash. `app.html` keeps `class="dark"`, so a visitor without JavaScript - or with storage blocked, where that script throws, having no try/catch (`setInitialMode` in its `dist/mode.js`) - still gets dark. Its `themeColors` prop also writes a `theme-color` meta, which the site has none of yet.
- [ ] **The toggle** has two states, dark and light, through `toggleMode()`: no "system" choice, as the system's preference is ignored by design.
- [ ] **Stays dark in light mode:** the 404 page, by `class="dark"` on its wrapper, which scopes the dark tokens to it - its starfield is `mix-blend-screen` over the page (`lost-in-space.svelte`), which on a light page washes out to white. The preview images, which `post-preview.ts` draws from the `.dark` block: an image shown in someone else's feed, not the reader's page.
- [ ] **The nav's logo** sits on a dark backdrop of its own in light mode: its swoosh is `#f8fafa` (`logo-mark.svg`), which vanishes on a light page.
- [ ] **A unit test of the theme's contrast:** it reads both colour blocks of `layout.css` and checks every pair of text and background the site uses against [WCAG's 4.5:1](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), so a colour changed later can't fall below it unnoticed. The code's colours are Shiki's theme, not the site's, and may be an exception.
- [ ] **End-to-end:** a page opens dark with the system asking for light (`colorScheme: 'light'`), and the toggle's choice survives a reload.
- [ ] **Design phase, from live demos** on the real pages, switched in the URL; the switcher goes before merging. The colours are oklch, the greens keeping their hue (`layout.css` already uses [`oklch()`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/color_value/oklch)), each text colour computed to clear 4.5:1 on the surfaces it sits on. `layout.css` keeps Mist's light values, unused, which have no greens and none of the site's own tokens.
  - **Decide the neutrals,** with the greens recomputed for each: A, near-white page, `oklch(0.987 0.002 197.1)` - the dark theme's text colour - with white cards; B, page and cards tinted with the primary's hue, `oklch(0.975 0.012 166.5)` and `oklch(0.995 0.004 166.5)`; C, grey page, `oklch(0.955 0.004 214.3)`, with white cards. The primary green as text comes out at about `oklch(0.525 0.108 166.5)`, `#017e5c`, 5.1:1 on white, and white text on it 5.1:1 as a button.
  - **Decide the link hover:** the lime kept at its hue has to darken to an olive, `oklch(0.53 0.136 126.6)`, `#577900`, to clear 4.5:1 on white; beside it the harmonies with the green - complementary raspberry `#d00393`, split-complementary violet `#af2dd7` or coral `#dd0045`. The analogous hues differ from the green links by hue alone, at the same lightness, and would barely show as a hover.
  - **Decide the mark** behind `@anonymus09`: kept as in dark, dark green with lime, 5.8:1 inside and 7.3:1 against the light page; or mint, `oklch(0.93 0.078 166.9)`, with dark green text, 4.56:1.
  - **Decide the code themes.** Measured on the posts' 21 code blocks, the share of characters under 4.5:1: on white, `github-light-default` and `light-plus` 0%, `github-light` 1%, every other light theme Shiki 4.5.0 bundles 17 to 98%; on today's dark block, `monokai` 46% (its pink keywords at 4.09:1), `dark-plus` 0.9%, `github-dark-default` 0%. The pairs to demo: `monokai` with `light-plus`, and `github-dark-default` with `github-light-default`, the one pair clear in both modes. Shiki writes both themes as CSS variables with [`defaultColor: false`](https://shiki.style/guide/dual-themes) - its docs show it with `codeToHtml`; the `highlighter.ts` that `add code-block` generates reads them from each token's `htmlStyle` with `codeToTokens`. The Code Block's background is `dark:bg-input/30` (`code-block.svelte` in `src/lib/components/ui/code-block/`), dark only, so light needs its own.
  - **Decide the toggle's place:** in the nav capsule beside the links, in the phone menu below `sm:`; or in the footer's row beside RSS and Source.
  - **Decide the backdrop** of transparent covers and project logos: today `bg-foreground` (`cover.ts`, `project-card.svelte`, `featured-project-card.svelte`), which turns dark in light mode - a new token, white in both modes, or the light theme's muted shade, or another light shade apart from the page's.
  - **Decide the hover glows,** `foreground` at 4 to 6% in `config.ts`, a dark haze on a light page: the primary green, a darker glow, or off in light mode.
