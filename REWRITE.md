# Rewrite plan

What is left of the site's rewrite: the features and the pages. The text they show - the posts, the projects and About - is the README's "Content" list - the new pages too, each built once its text is written - and what waits for another project is its "Waiting on upstream".

Each group below is one pull request, in this order; a big one can split. A checkbox is a task. **Decide** marks a choice to make before the tasks that depend on it. Facts were checked on 2026-10-24: claims about this repository name the file they come from, and claims about anything else link their source.

## 1. Light mode

Dark stays the default for everyone, whatever their system asks for; light is an option for accessibility or a strong preference, chosen with a toggle.

- [ ] **Wiring:** [`mode-watcher`](https://github.com/svecosystem/mode-watcher) 1.1.0, the latest, as shadcn-svelte's [dark mode docs](https://shadcn-svelte.com/docs/dark-mode/svelte) use: `<ModeWatcher defaultMode="dark" />` in the root layout. Its head script sets `.dark` and `color-scheme` on `<html>` from `localStorage` before the first paint, so a prerendered page doesn't flash. `app.html` keeps `class="dark"`, so a visitor without JavaScript - or with storage blocked, where that script throws, having no try/catch (`setInitialMode` in its `dist/mode.js`) - still gets dark. Its `themeColors` prop also writes a `theme-color` meta, which the site has none of yet.
- [ ] **The toggle** has two states, dark and light, through `toggleMode()`: no "system" choice, as the system's preference is ignored by design.
- [ ] **Stays dark in light mode:** the 404 page, by `class="dark"` on its wrapper, which scopes the dark tokens to it - its starfield is `mix-blend-screen` over the page (`lost-in-space.svelte`), which on a light page washes out to white. The preview images, which `post-preview.ts` draws from the `.dark` block: an image shown in someone else's feed, not the reader's page.
- [ ] **The nav's logo** in light mode is a copy, `logo-mark-light.svg`, with its swoosh in the light theme's text colour: in `logo-mark.svg` it is `#f8fafa`, which vanishes on a light page. A dark or a mid-grey circle behind the white one was tried and rejected.
- [ ] **The nav's glass** in light mode, decided from demos: as in dark, but lighter - the text colour at 8%, its shadow at 15% and the current page's pill at 10%, as `--nav-glass`, `--nav-shadow` and `--nav-current`, over a glass tinted with the primary. In dark they are the classes the capsule had.
- [ ] **A unit test of the theme's contrast:** it reads both colour blocks of `layout.css` and checks every pair of text and background the site uses against [WCAG's 4.5:1](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), so a colour changed later can't fall below it unnoticed. The code's colours are Shiki's theme, not the site's, and may be an exception.
- [ ] **End-to-end:** a page opens dark with the system asking for light (`colorScheme: 'light'`), and the toggle's choice survives a reload.
- [ ] **Design phase, from live demos** on the real pages, switched in the URL; the switcher goes before merging. The colours are oklch, the greens keeping their hue (`layout.css` already uses [`oklch()`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/color_value/oklch)), each text colour computed to clear 4.5:1 on the surfaces it sits on. The decided ones are in `layout.css`'s `:root`; the options still open are in `src/lib/components/demo/`.
  - [ ] **The neutrals,** decided from the demos: a grey page, `oklch(0.955 0.004 214.3)`, with white cards, Mist's light greys darkened to match, in `layout.css`'s `:root`. The primary green as text is `oklch(0.51 0.104 166.5)`, 4.78:1 on the page and 5.44:1 on a card, and white text on it 5.44:1 as a button.
  - [ ] **The link hover,** decided: the lime at its hue, darkened to an olive, `oklch(0.515 0.132 126.6)`, 4.75:1 on the page - over the harmonies, raspberry, violet and coral.
  - [ ] **The mark** behind `@anonymus09`, decided: kept as in dark, dark green with lime, 5.8:1 inside and 7.3:1 against the light page - over mint with dark green text, and the link hover's olive as its text, 1.4:1.
  - [ ] **The code themes,** decided from demos: `monokai` stays for dark, and `one-light` goes with it for light, chosen for being colourful, as Monokai is. Neither clears 4.5:1 on every character, so contrast informed the choice without ruling themes out. Measured on the posts' 21 code blocks: `monokai` on the dark block (`#202426`) has its comments at 4.16:1 and its pink keywords at 4.14:1, 46% of the characters, all above 3:1; `one-light` on white has 73% under 4.5:1 and 40% under 3:1, its pale grey comments. GitHub's themes and `light-plus` pass everywhere but were turned down as too plain; a Monokai with those two colours lightened to 4.5:1 was tried too. Shiki writes both themes into every token as `--shiki-dark` and `--shiki-light` with [`defaultColor: false`](https://shiki.style/guide/dual-themes), which the post's `code-block.svelte` picks by the dark class. The Code Block's own background is `dark:bg-input/30` (`code-block.svelte` in `src/lib/components/ui/code-block/`), dark only, so in light the code sits on the white card.
  - [ ] **The toggle's place,** decided: in the footer, at the page's right edge, apart from its centred line - over the nav capsule. On the home page the footer stays at the foot of the screen, the page scrolling behind it, by `sticky`.
  - [ ] **The backdrop** of transparent covers and project logos, decided: a `--backdrop` token, the foreground in dark as `bg-foreground` was (`cover.ts`, `project-card.svelte`, `featured-project-card.svelte`), and in light the card's own white, outlined by a 1px inset ring in the border colour - light mode only, so dark is unchanged. The muted grey was chosen first and walked back: the cover's brightening on hover stood out too much on it.
  - [ ] **The hover glows,** decided: the cards' own `foreground`, at 4 to 6% in `config.ts`, twice as strong in light mode, by `--glow-boost`, which the glow utility in `layout.css` multiplies the strength by - at the same strength the dark haze barely showed on a white card. The primary green and the olive were tried.
